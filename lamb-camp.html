Lamb Camp

Experience bottle feeding our baby lambs.

Step into our lamb nursery

Some of our young lambs depend on hand feedings throughout the day and need a little extra care. We open that daily routine to you and your family. Hold the bottle, feed a lamb, and watch those little tails wag with excitement.

It's hands-on farm life, the way we've come to know and love it, and a perfect way to make family memories.

Good to know

  • Lamb Camp runs during lambing season in the spring. Contact us for available dates.
  • Every visitor signs a waiver before entering the farm.
  • Great for families and birthdays. Ask us about large groups and class trips.

A note on safety: Ewes can carry infections that pass to their lambs and can pose a risk to expectant mothers. We ask that pregnant visitors sit this one out, but it's up to your discretion.
