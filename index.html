Our story

A husband and wife farm in Prentiss

Tilton's Heritage Pastures is our family farm in the small town of Prentiss, Maine. We started it in 2020 and are committed to farming practices that care for the land and keep it healthy for the generations after us.

Al handles the fields and the flock, Deborah manages the business, and we both treat our animals like family. From morning chores in the barn to evening rounds in the garden, there is nothing quite like the pride of bringing fresh, honest food to your table.
