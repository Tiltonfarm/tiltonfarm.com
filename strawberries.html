From the high tunnel

Farm fresh strawberries

Sweet, ripe berries grown under cover in Prentiss, Maine.

Grown for flavor

We grow Albion strawberries, a day-neutral variety that keeps producing berries through the summer and into fall instead of one short June crop. Growing them in our high tunnel protects the plants from heavy rain and cold nights, so the berries ripen evenly and stay clean.

Every berry is picked by hand when it's fully red and ready to eat.

  • Albion day-neutral variety
  • Grown in a high tunnel
  • Hand picked at full ripeness
  • Summer through early fall
